Chirp to Baofeng DM-32 Converter

Turn a Chirp CSV export into a channel CSV the Baofeng DM-32 CPS can import. Everything runs in your browser. Nothing is uploaded.

How the fields are mapped
  • TX frequency is computed from Chirp's Duplex and Offset. + and - add or subtract the offset, split uses Offset as the absolute TX frequency, blank or off copies RX.
  • Duplex off (receive only) sets Forbid TX to 1 so the radio cannot transmit there.
  • Mode NFM becomes 12.5KHz, FM becomes 25KHz.
  • Power at or above the threshold becomes High, otherwise Low. The DM-32 has two levels.
  • Tones: Tone, TSQL, DTCS and Cross modes are mapped into CTC/DCS Encode and Decode. CTCSS is written like 100.0, DCS like D023N or D023I.
  • Everything else uses plain analog defaults matching a DM-32 CPS export: Carrier/CTC squelch, no scan list, no APRS, Slot 1, colour code 0.
  • Skip and Comment are dropped. The DM-32 handles scanning through scan lists.
After importing: zones

The DM-32 only lets you select channels that belong to the current zone. Importing channels does not put them in a zone, so after the import open the Zone tab in the CPS and add the channels. Each zone holds up to 64 channels, so more than 64 channels need at least two zones. If the radio wraps back to channel 1 early, the zone is the reason.

The DM-32 is a VHF/UHF radio. Channels outside 136 to 174 MHz and 400 to 480 MHz, such as 1.25 m ham channels, will be flagged below and may be rejected by the CPS.

Also available as a command line script: chirp2dm32.py on GitHub.
